When Earth faces its darkest hour, a team of extraordinary heroes rises to defend it. Iron Man leads with genius and powered armor. Thor, god of thunder, brings the force of storms. Captain America stands for courage and justice. The Hulk unleashes unstoppable strength. Black Widow and Hawkeye strike with deadly skill. Together they are the Avengers, mightiest protectors of our world. No villain is too great when heroes fight as one.
